# Unwritten Rules in every workplace nobody tells you about 

1.Protected people in the workplace that work with you.
  - they are protected and shielded from accountability, even when their performance drags down everybody else.
  - they are protected by connections. Sometimes referred as flying monkeys/psychophants of the management.
  - they are tools to create triggers to suit management outcomes

2. Informal power structure at work
  - they are invisible, not the people on the org chart, but the people who are actually making things happen working in shadow
  - sometimes they are referred as lapdog or attack dog
  - the org chart is just for show. it's a horse and pony show.
  - the job titles tell you who reports to who. it doesn't necessarily tell you who shapes the big decisions in the company

3. The change agent
   - when the narrative states that you are being brought in to shape things up and to help them change things and turn the company around.
   - that's all good and dandy until you touch someone that somebody powerful has built. that finding will gradually point who the power players are and how you made them feel uncomfortable.
